We conducted a simulation study with  15600 replications. Simulation conditions were  list(reps = 1:100, k = c(20, 50, 100, 300), classes = 2L, vi = c(0.001,  0.01, 0.1), tau2_ratio = c(2, 4, 6), p_ratio = c(1, 1.5)) , chosen to align with the assumptions of metaplus.

In  85.71 % of replications, the remix model had a lower minus 2 log likelihood than the metaplus model, indicating that it found a better solution.

The difference in bias between the two methods was small for all parameters: mu: -0.01 tau: -0.04 pi: -0.02

The methods had near-identical confidence interval coverage for mu, but metaplus did not return confidence intervals for tau2 or pi in any replication.
